Nozzle Physics & Efficiency

Water-Efficient Solutions & Automatic Sprinkler Highlights

Every drop is calculated. Our precision-engineered nozzles create wind-resistant droplet sizes that reach soil roots instead of evaporating into thin air.

Model: AQUA-ROTARY-360 PRN 35 PSI

Brass core pivot with matched precipitation gear drive

Matched Precipitation Rotaries (MPR)

Replaces misting sprays with multi-trajectory rotating streams at 0.4 in/hr, matching soil percolation speed perfectly.

Pressure-Regulated Stems (PRS)

Locks dynamic pressure at optimal 30-40 PSI, preventing pressure spikes that cause atomized water fogs and drift loss.

Subsurface Porous Drip Tubing

Embedded directly into the root layer for zero evaporation, zero wind interruption, and 100% direct nutrient absorption.

Seal-A-Matic (SAM) Check Valves

Holds back up to 14 ft of elevation head pressure, eliminating post-cycle drainage onto sidewalks and erosion at hill bases.
